MBA 2K 27

Original 3v3 street basketball. Every city, every color, all-new players.

3v3 third-person basketball. Pick two city teams in their colors; every player is an original.

WASD – Move
Shift – Sprint
Space – Hold to shoot, release in the green window (close to the rim it is a layup)
X – Ankle breaker (crossover)
C – Spin move
V – Dunk; tap V again in the green for a perfect dunk (posterizes defenders)
Z – Hold stepback, release in the green
R – Rebound jump     B – Block jump
T / G / H – Between legs / Behind back / Hesitation (chain moves for combos)
M – Hold fadeaway, release in green     L – Double clutch layup
E – Pass     F – Steal     Q – Switch defender
Space on defense – Jump and block
WASD: move   Shift: sprint
