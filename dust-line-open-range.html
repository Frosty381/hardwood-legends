Dust Line Open Range

Ride the open frontier, gather the scattered corpse parts for their powers, awaken a fighter to stand beside you, and face the Corpse King when the last part is found.

Touch: use the buttons and drag on the view to look around. Keyboard: W/A/S/D, Space, Shift, E, C for camera, Q and Z/X/V for your fighter, G to fish, M for the map, N to mute the music, P to pause.

Corpse parts and powers

  • Heart, Thunder Hooves: +70% speed for 10 seconds.
  • Ribs, Gale Stride: +40% speed and a higher jump for 15 seconds.
  • Left Eye, Sun Shoe: +8% speed for good, up to 60%, and full stamina.
  • Spine, Iron Hide: half damage for 20 seconds.
  • Skull, Hawk Eye: +60% gun damage and easier hits for 25 seconds.
  • Right Hand, Bull Rush: plough through cacti, rocks and trees for 15 seconds.
  • Fang, Lodestone: nearby corpse parts fly toward you for 25 seconds.
  • Lungs, Second Wind: heal 40 at once, then keep healing for 10 seconds.
  • Left Foot, Ghost Step: outlaws cannot spot you for 20 seconds.
  • Left Hand, Slow Sand: every foe at half speed for 12 seconds.
  • Jawbone, Storm Call: lightning strikes the four nearest foes.
  • Gold Tooth, Midas Tooth: +60 coins, and outlaws pay double for 45 seconds.
  • There are 51 parts on the range. The Corpse King still rises at 22.

Out on the range

  • Twelve corpse parts sit inside rings of cacti. A horse is too wide to enter, so press E, walk through the gap, then press E beside your horse to remount.
  • Six quest givers keep camps spread across the range. Press M for the full map, ride out to a camp and press T to talk.
  • Tornadoes only form while you are in the red desert. They drag you in and throw you around, so keep your distance.
  • Collect the last corpse part and the Corpse King rises. Beat him to win.
  • On foot at the edge of a lake, face the water and press G to fish: time the cast, hit the key that flashes up when the fish bites, then press the T, D, G and H keys in the order shown to reel it in. The order changes every time.
  • Crash into something at a gallop and you are thrown from the saddle. Press E beside your horse to climb back on.
  • Bears roam the woods and will charge you. Rancher Mae's quest leads to their cave, where Ironclaw sleeps.
  • Two more towns, Copper Gulch and Saltbrook, lie far out on the bigger range. Every town has a gunsmith, a trading post and a bank.
  • On foot at a bank door, press H to crack the safe, then escape the deputies with the loot. Rob three and Marshal Graves comes for you.
  • Outlaw squads of five ride together, wild mustangs and buffalo roam the plains, and the Storm Altar calls the Thunderbird for 3 Hawk Plumes.
  • Worlds, on the start menu, keeps separate save files. Make a new world and switch back whenever you like.
  • Day turns to night in about five minutes. Nights are moonlit, and corpse part beams glow brighter after dark.

Guns, furs and your fighter

  • Eleven guns are for sale at the gunsmith in any town (press B nearby), and two more drop from bosses. Click or press F to fire, R reloads, and the number keys, [ and ] or the mouse wheel swap guns. Headshots do double damage.
  • Shoot an animal to bring it down, then walk up on foot, press K and hit the T, D, G and H keys in the order shown to skin it with the skinning knife (40 coins at any Trading Post). Sell the hides at the Trading Post, the second tab of the town shop.
  • Each corpse part has a 5% chance to awaken a fighter, and some quests pay one. Press Q to choose one of twelve. You can only have one, it attacks on its own, and Z, X and V trigger its three moves. Q sends it away and calls it back, and the Stable lets you recolour, resize and rename it.
  • Change your horse and outfit in the Stable, from the start or pause menu. The Platinum Rider trophy unlocks a platinum horse, platinum clothes and a rider badge.
